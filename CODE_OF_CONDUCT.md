# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Giving and accepting feedback on the work rather than the person.
- Saying what you actually tried, what happened, and what you expected — a bug
  report with the real output is worth more than a polished one without it.
- Being clear about uncertainty instead of asserting past what you know.
- Respecting that maintainer time is finite, and that "no" to a feature is not a
  judgement of the person asking.

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — including about
  someone's experience level, background, or identity.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to relitigate them,
  or demanding unpaid work as though it were owed.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **daniel.arrizza@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately
and in confidence. The maintainer will respond as soon as reasonably possible
and may edit or remove content, or block accounts, when that is the
proportionate response.

This project is small and maintained by one person, so enforcement is a
judgement call rather than a process. The intent is straightforward: keep this a
place where people are willing to report a bug or disagree in public.
